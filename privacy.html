Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

About this app

PinShop Hub is a private tool I built and use only to manage my own Pinterest ad account. It is not offered, sold or made available to anyone else.

Data the app accesses

With the ads:read permission, the app reads only data from my own ad account:

  • Campaign names, status and daily budgets
  • Daily performance metrics: spend, impressions, clicks, outbound clicks and saves

The app is read-only. It does not create, edit or publish anything on Pinterest, and it does not access other users' data.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show reports and to help me analyze and compare my own campaigns. To write these analyses, parts of the data may be processed on my behalf by an AI service (Anthropic's Claude).

Storage and sharing

The data is stored locally on my own computer. It is never sold, rented or shared with third parties for advertising or any other purpose.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time in the Pinterest account settings. I can delete the locally stored data at any time.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/foaxisz/pinshop-hub/issues.
